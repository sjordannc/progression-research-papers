# Research papers by Scott A. Jordan

This repository collects three distinct research manuscripts. They are shared as drafts for reading and discussion; their inclusion here does not imply peer review or empirical validation.

| Manuscript | Files | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Progression Geometry as a Complete Gravitational Field** (July 2026) | [PDF](progression_geometry.pdf) · [LaTeX source](progression_geometry.tex) | Main progression-geometry theory, including the Einstein benchmark, infrared deformation, stability, post-Newtonian limit, and FLRW cosmology. This is the latest version in this collection. |
| **From Relational Progression to the Hypersurface-Deformation Algebra** (August 2026) | [PDF](progression_weighted_conservation.pdf) · [LaTeX source](progression_weighted_conservation.tex) | A conditional route from relational clock progression to Einstein geometrodynamics and shared matter characteristic cones. |
| **Collective Phase Loading and Progression Exchange in Progression-Weighted Matter Dynamics** (July 2026) | [PDF](collective_phase_loading.pdf) · [LaTeX source](collective_phase_loading.tex) | Matter-sector companion referenced by the interaction-complexity paper; develops collective loading, progression exchange, and conservation identities. |
| **Interaction Complexity as a Covariant Source for Progression Geometry** (draft, July 2026) | [PDF](interaction_complexity_progression_geometry.pdf) · [LaTeX source](interaction_complexity_progression_geometry.tex) | A separate two-current source-sector proposal and its consistency conditions. |
| **Relational Spectral Inertia from Cosmological Frame Dragging** (draft, September 2026) | [PDF](relational_spectral_inertia_draft.pdf) · [LaTeX source](relational_spectral_inertia_draft.tex) | A speculative modified-inertia framework, distinct from the progression-geometry source model. |

The progression-geometry PDF and source both include the FLRW section. The separate [weighted-conservation repository](https://github.com/sjordannc/weighted-conservation) contains related galaxy-test data and code.
